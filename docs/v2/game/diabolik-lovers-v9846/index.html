Komori Yui is an average high school student. However, since she was young, odd things have been happening around her.

When she started her second year of high school, she had to change schools due to her father changing jobs.

"Ryoutei High School" is a night school for performers and celebrities. Although held in high regard, it was plagued by negative rumors. Among them was one that a student had been attacked by a vampire...

The Sakamaki estate is infamous in town for being the haunted home to six awful brothers...

Suddenly, Yui moves in with these same six attractive brothers. Something is off about them...

Though she's filled with unease and fear, she tries to start her new life.
