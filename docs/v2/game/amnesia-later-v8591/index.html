Fandisk to the original game. There’s “New World” which is like another story to the original story where the heroine still has no memories. The After Story takes place after she regains her memories in the original game and then there’s other bonuses like Waka World and Girls Party. Additionally you get a chance to reflect on your past journey with Orion.
