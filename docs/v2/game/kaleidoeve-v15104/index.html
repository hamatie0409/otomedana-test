This is an organization that protects the people that live in its country.

Two new troops were recruited into CUE this spring, which is a special forces unit composed of the best and most talented members from the S.A.D.F.

One of them is 18 year old Tsubasa Hayama. Even though she was a bit lost as to why she was chosen to be assigned to CUE, every day her connection with the other members deepened.

Around that time a series of incidents of random street attacks started. In order to resolve the situation the members of CUE were dispatched.
