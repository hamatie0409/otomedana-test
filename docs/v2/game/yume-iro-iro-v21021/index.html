ストーリー

日本語のあらすじは未収録です。VNDB の英語原文を表示しています。

A Tokimeki Memorial inspired title where the protagonist can be either male (Yumeiro Tarou) or female (Yumeiro Hanako). For three years, they will build connections with their classmates and sometimes, they will meet them in 'Dream Scenarios', alternative realities that happen while they sleep.

All female characters were voiced by talents represented by the Feathered agency at the time.
