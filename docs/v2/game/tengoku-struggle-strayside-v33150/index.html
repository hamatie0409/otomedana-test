ストーリー

日本語のあらすじは未収録です。VNDB の英語原文を表示しています。

It is year XX of the Reiwa era—

Through an agreement between the Human Realm and the Hell Realm, Asakusa was transformed into a tourist attraction where the living can meet the dead. One day, the Spider's Thread was dropped from Heaven down into Hell, allowing many of Hell's prisoners to escape.

King Enma thus orders his novice Hell Guardian daughter, the protagonist, to capture the escapees along with Goemon Ishikawa and a few other trustworthy prisoners. The protagonist's group immediately descends upon the Human Realm to monitor the escaped prisoners. During their mission, they attend an event led by former prisoner of Hell, Shinobikuni, where she reveals a pardoning app that will forgive people of their sins. What is Shinobikuni's goal? And what will become of the humans who were promised entry into Heaven?

This is a story that encompasses Heaven and Earth – what fate awaits this world?
