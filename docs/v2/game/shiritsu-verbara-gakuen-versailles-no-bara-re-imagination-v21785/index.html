VerRose Academy - the famous private school everyone dreams of attending. A girl named Yamada Oskar is about to walk through its doors.

At the entrance ceremony performance, when she sees the dazzling "Oscar" on stage in a gorgeous costume, her heart begins to pound like an alarm bell.

"Is this... love!?"

All set to fight for the championship! Or so she thought - instead, she finds herself surrounded by eccentric personalities, caught up in sudden major incidents, and even pressured to switch clubs!?
