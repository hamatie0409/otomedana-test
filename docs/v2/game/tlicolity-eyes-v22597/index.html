ストーリー

日本語のあらすじは未収録です。VNDB の英語原文を表示しています。

Tlicolity Eyes is a part of "triAngle PROJECT," a collaboration project between Otomate and Frontier Works. Each series will have 3 games released in span of 9 months for Windows under the same concept: one heroine, two heroes, and triangle love.

Tlicolity Eyes theme is entertainment industry x love comedy x triangle love.

[Story]

――This is a story a bit ahead in the future.

Approximately 30% of the population have a magical power and are called the "Ability Users." It is rumored that many of ability users are shining in the world of entertainment.

The heroines of the story―the Touchi Sisters―are also one of them, and each of them are struggling in the world of entertainment!

Their ability is a very convenient ability that [temporarily upgrades someone else's status].

However, there's a secret behind that ability.

It's a side effect called "Tlicolity" that makes the person who gets the effect becomes in love with the girls!

A triangle relationship [ToriAi] as a side effect to the entertainment world!

Is this throbbing also a side effect? Or――
