ストーリー

日本語のあらすじは未収録です。VNDB の英語原文を表示しています。

This is the fandisk to the original “Starry Sky~In Autumn~” continuing after the normal ending in the original game. Tsukiko comes back to Seigatsu Gakuen after a number of years as a trainee teacher and meets up with the original gang – her old home-room teacher Naoshi, school doctor Kotarou and newly qualified teacher Iku. Now they enjoy life at school while Tsukiko is on her 3 month placement. Of course, this game will also have after stories for all 3 original main characters and I suspect that there will be endings for some of the sub-characters in the original game too.

[Taken from Liemyx's blog]
