A fandisc that includes several stories:

- Lover's Day: Follows either Airi or Luna after the story of the first game, seeing what happened after they made the choice of going back to their world or staying in Wonderland.

- Another Line: a "what if" story that follows the original queen of Wonderland Stella and her beloved Humpty.
